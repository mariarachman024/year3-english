Grammar Notes

Superlative Adjectives Rules & Examples

💡 What is a Superlative Adjective?

We use a superlative adjective when we compare one person, animal or thing with a whole group (3 or more).

📌 Important Rule: Usually, we put "the" before a superlative adjective!

🐾 Animal Comparison Example

Smallest

The cat is the smallest.

Fastest

The cheetah is the fastest.

Biggest

The elephant is the biggest.

6 Simple Grammar Rules to Remember:

Rule 1

Short Adjectives: add "-est"

tall ➔ the tallest
small ➔ the smallest
fast ➔ the fastest
Rule 2

Ending in "-e": add "-st"

large ➔ the largest
nice ➔ the nicest
Rule 3

Consonant + Vowel + Consonant: Double final letter + "-est"

big ➔ the biggest (double 'g')
hot ➔ the hottest (double 't')
Rule 4

Ending in consonant + "y": Change "y" to "i" + "-est"

happy ➔ the happiest
funny ➔ the funniest
easy ➔ the easiest
Rule 5

Longer Adjectives: Use "the most"

beautiful ➔ the most beautiful
interesting ➔ the most interesting
expensive ➔ the most expensive
Rule 6

Irregular Adjectives (Special words!)

good ➔ the best
bad ➔ the worst
far ➔ the farthest
